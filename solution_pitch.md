NAMMAVOICE – AI-POWERED MULTILINGUAL CITIZEN GRIEVANCE PLATFORM

Tagline:Your voice. Your issue. Our attention.

Problem
Many citizens face difficulties reporting civic problems due to language barriers, complicated complaint procedures, and difficulty explaining issues in writing.

Solution
NammaVoice is a multilingual AI-powered civic grievance platform that allows citizens to register local complaints through voice or text in Kannada, English, Hindi, Telugu, and Tamil.

How It Works
1. Citizens speak or type their complaints.
2. Gnani AI Prisma STT converts speech into text and supports language detection.
3. The application analyses complaints and suggests the responsible department and severity.
4. Citizens can optionally attach their location, contact details, and photo evidence.
5. A unique complaint ID is generated for tracking.
6. Administrators can review complaints, update status, and provide remarks.
7. Gnani AI Timbre TTS provides multilingual voice confirmations.

Gnani AI Models Used
- Prisma STT: Multilingual speech-to-text transcription.
- Timbre TTS: Text-to-speech voice responses.

Target Users
Citizens reporting local civic issues and administrators managing complaints.

Expected Impact
NammaVoice aims to reduce language barriers, make complaint registration more accessible, encourage citizen participation, and improve complaint tracking and administration.

Technology
Python, Flask, SQLite, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Gnani AI Prisma STT and Timbre TTS.
